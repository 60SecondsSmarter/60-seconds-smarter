About 60 Seconds Smarter

60 Seconds Smarter is an educational short-video channel on TikTok and YouTube. Every video explains one free AI tool, a practical tech trick or a smart everyday hack in about a minute.

Our desktop studio

The videos are produced with 60 Seconds Smarter Studio, a private desktop application that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It helps research trending topics, write scripts, create AI-assisted visuals and voice-overs, and publish the finished videos to the channel owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts using the official APIs.

The studio is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. All AI-generated content is labeled as such using the platforms' official AI-content settings.
