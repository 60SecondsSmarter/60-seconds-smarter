Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Summary

60 Seconds Smarter Studio (the "App") is a private desktop application used only by the channel operator. It does not collect data from viewers or other users, and it does not sell or share any data.

2. Data the App processes

When the channel operator connects their own TikTok or YouTube account, the App receives through the official APIs:

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok or Google (to act on the operator's own account).
  • Basic profile information of the operator's account: display name, username, avatar, follower/subscriber, like and video counts.
  • Statistics of the operator's own published videos: views, likes, comments and shares.

This information is used only to publish the operator's videos and to show performance statistics inside the App.

3. Where data is stored

All data, including tokens, is stored locally on the operator's own computer. There is no server, no cloud database and no third-party analytics. Data is not shared with anyone.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected in the App. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google account settings.

5. Website

This website uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics.
